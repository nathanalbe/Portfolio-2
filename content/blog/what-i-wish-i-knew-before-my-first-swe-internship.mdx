Internships compress years of professional texture into a few months. Here is what I wish someone had said earlier.

## Ask for the shape of success

“Do good work” is not a spec. In the first week, ask:

- What does a strong outcome look like for this project?
- Who reviews my work?
- How do we measure impact?

## Write things down

Decisions evaporate. A short weekly note — what shipped, what blocked, what you learned — compounds into a performance story you can actually tell later.

## Own a thin slice

Volunteer for something end-to-end, even if small. Ownership teaches more than watching a large system from the sidelines.

## Feedback is a skill

Request it early. Make it easy to give. Treat it as data, not identity.

## The real curriculum

Syntax is the easy part. Communication, prioritization, and judgment under ambiguity are the internship.
