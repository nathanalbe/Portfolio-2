Founding a chapter sounds glamorous until you are the person booking rooms, writing outreach messages, and hoping people show up.

## Why start

Too many students were preparing for tech careers in isolation — strong coursework, weak community. ColorStack gave us a proven mission and a network. We still had to make it real on campus.

## What actually worked

- **Consistency over hype.** Regular events beat one big launch.
- **Industry partners as co-teachers.** Visa, Deloitte, AWS, WillowTree, and ETS helped turn “networking” into concrete practice.
- **Students owning the room.** Leadership scales when people get real responsibility early.

## Numbers that matter

We grew to **150+ members** after founding in **2024**. The metric I care about more is whether someone left an event with a clearer next step — resume review, interview practice, or a peer who would check in.

## Still learning

Community work is product work: listen, ship small, iterate. The chapter is unfinished on purpose. That is the point.
